## How to add yourself

For named roles (Clinical Advisor, Translation Lead, Security Reviewer): a maintainer adds you when the role is confirmed.

For Translators: open a PR adding your line under "Translators" with your locale and a sentence about what you contributed.

For Security Hall of Fame: a maintainer adds you after coordinated disclosure.
